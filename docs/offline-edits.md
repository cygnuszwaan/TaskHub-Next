# Offline: held edits, Reopen and comments

How the page holds the changes made in forms without a connection (or behind changes already waiting) and sends them in order, on the action queue in `offline.md`. The plan for the remaining ones is the roadmap in PROJECT.md.

- Reopen (page): `reopenCompletedEntry` confirms (`reopenPrompt`), then fixes a `requestId` and tries the calls as online (the form's unsaved edits to `updateCompletedTask`, then `{id, requestId}` to `reopenCompletedTask`). On a connection failure it calls `queueReopen` with the fields not yet saved, and at once, with `behind`, while any action is PENDING or a drain runs. `queueReopen` keeps an EDIT of the changed fields `ENTRY_EDIT_FIELDS` holds (when the form changed any) and then a REOPEN (`taskId` the entry's own `id`, `archiveId` the entry's), refusing with an alert that names the other changed fields (`splitHeldFields`, `fieldNamesText`: they need a connection) or when the queue has no room; it then closes the form and shows the result (`refreshShownTasks`): the entry leaves History and the task joins the end of the Queue as `reopenTask` builds it. `shownTasks` replays over `replayEntries` (the page's History entries, else the saved copy's, read only while a REOPEN waits) because a REOPEN finds its entry there, so the task shows on a cold start before History has been opened. `sendQueuedAction` posts a REOPEN's `reopenBody` to `reopenCompletedTask` and an entry EDIT's `entryEditBody` to `updateCompletedTask`. A completion that is still waiting is not reopened but taken back (`takeBackWaitingCompletion`, `takeBackCompletion`): its COMPLETE leaves the queue with the actions that depend on it, and a timer that was running is paused at the completion's `at` (a TIMER PAUSE action), so the task is back stopped with its accrued time and nothing is sent. Reopen has no Undo; discarding a waiting REOPEN (`cancelActions`) also drops later actions on its task but leaves the entry's EDIT, which has its own card.

- Held task edits (page): `saveTaskEdits` closes the form and posts as online; on a connection failure (`isNetworkError`), or before posting while any action is PENDING or a drain runs, `holdTaskEdit` keeps one EDIT of the fields that differ from the task shown (`taskEditChanges`, so an untouched field is never sent and an edit made on another device meanwhile is overwritten only in the fields changed here): title, priority, estimatedMinutes, tags, dueDate, recurrence, travelKm, phone, email, location and description. `checkEditFields` cleans each value as the server stores it (`normalizeSnooze`, `normalizeTravelKm`, `normalizePhone`, `normalizeEmail`, `normalizeLocation`, `normalizeRecurrence` are page copies of the server's, held equal by `actionQueue.test.js`, as is `applyEditFields` against `applyDirectUpdate`) and names an unusable value in an alert. A change to materials, the project, its order number or the task number (`serverOnlyEditChanges`) is refused with an alert that names it and nothing is kept; the form comes back (`reopenEditForm`) when the refusal follows a failed post. Snooze (`snoozeTask`) holds the same way through `holdSnooze`: an EDIT of `snoozedUntil` (null unsnoozes). `sendQueuedAction` posts a task EDIT's `taskEditBody` (`updateTaskDirect` UPDATE of the held fields) and, after a held snooze is saved, runs `rescheduleTask` when `snoozeNeedsReschedule` says the task has an event to move. The cards show the change at once (`withPendingActions`) and the action card's label names the fields (`actionLabel`).

- Held entry edits (page): `saveCompletedEdit` posts as online; on a connection failure (`isNetworkError`), or before posting while any action is PENDING or a drain runs, or when the entry is a completion still waiting to be sent (`waitingRequestId`), `holdEntryEdit` keeps one EDIT of the changed fields (`completedChanges`, so only what differs from the entry shown) and closes the form; the entry shows the change at once and the card names the fields (`actionLabel`). A change to materials, the project or the completion time (`splitHeldFields`' other fields: they need ids or the zone the server holds) is refused with an alert that names it, nothing is kept and the form stays open. A waiting entry opens (`openCompletedEditor`, `completedFormLimited`) with only the held fields enabled and its photos, comments, job sheet and Delete disabled; its EDIT is sent after its COMPLETE and goes with it when the completion is discarded or taken back (`actionDependsOn`). `checkEditFields` cleans an entry's tags (`cleanEntryTags`) and time (`MAX_TRACKED_SECONDS`) as `applyCompletedEdit` does.

- Held comments (page): `postComment` posts as online; on a connection failure (`isNetworkError`), or before posting while any action is PENDING or a drain runs, or when the entry is a completion still waiting to be sent, `holdComment` keeps a COMMENT with the comment's own id (the one `commentIdFor` fixed, so a repeat adds nothing), its trimmed text and `at` (device clock plus `clockOffsetMs`, the time it was written, not the time it arrives), then clears the box and returns true, so a Save, Snooze or Complete that added it first goes on. The task card, the entry and the timeline show it at once (`withPendingActions`, `addCommentToList`) marked "Waiting to send" (`waitingCommentIds`); a task with a waiting COMMENT shows the waiting chip. A comment on a waiting entry is sent after its COMPLETE and goes with it when the completion is discarded or taken back; one on a task follows any REOPEN of it. `sendQueuedAction` posts `commentBody` to `addComment`; a server answer marks the action FAILED with its text (a comment more than 14 days old is refused), and the reply patches an open form (`applyCommentResult`, only for an entry the page holds). A full queue or unusable storage refuses with an alert and the text stays in the box. Deleting a comment (`deleteComment`) asks first, then: a comment still waiting in the queue is taken out of it and nothing is sent (unless its COMMENT is on its way, when the deletion goes behind it); otherwise the call is made as online and held as a COMMENT_DELETE (`holdCommentAction`, fields `{id}`) after a connection failure, behind waiting changes, or on an entry whose completion is waiting. The comment leaves the timeline, card and entry at once (`removeCommentFromList`, against the server's `removeComment`); `sendQueuedAction` posts `commentDeleteBody` to `deleteComment`, where a comment already gone is a harmless repeat. Its delete controls are never disabled.
