# Endpoints: History, photos and exports

The functions for completed tasks, their photos, templates, job sheets and exports. Every endpoint needs the owner's sign-in (see PROJECT.md).

| Function | Purpose | AI |
|---|---|---|
| `getCompletedTasks` | Read the newest entries of `completed_tasks`, newest first (`?limit=`, default 50, at most 1000; the page asks for 1000 and `listTruncated` says the list reached its limit), plus `summary` over the last 8 local weeks (Monday start, current week included, from up to 1000 archive rows regardless of `limit`): `totalCount`, `totalMinutes`, per-week, per-priority, per-tag and per-project (`{projectId, name, count, minutes}`, current project names; tasks of deleted projects share one entry with a null id and name; tasks outside any project are left out) `count` and `minutes`, and `byJob` (`{title, count, completedCount, averageMinutes, estimatedMinutes}`: one entry per title, ignoring case, completed at least twice in all, most completed in the window first; `count` is its completions in the window, `completedCount` the larger of the newest row's stored count and `count`, `averageMinutes` the rolling average of the newest row's `recentSeconds` (else of the tracked times of its rows in the window) rounded to 5 minutes or null, `estimatedMinutes` the newest row's own estimate or null) (the time tracked on each task, `trackedSeconds`, summed exactly and rounded to whole minutes; a row with no tracked time, whether never timed or archived before time tracking began, counts its estimate instead), `streak` (`{current, longest, currentLimited, longestLimited}`: runs of local days with a completion inside the window; the current run may end yesterday; a `Limited` flag means that run reaches the window's first day, so the number is a lower bound) and `truncated` when the window held more rows than were read; and, outside `summary` and not limited to its window, `projectHours` (`[{projectId, name, count, minutes, estimatedMinutes, weeks: [{weekStart, count, minutes, estimatedMinutes}]}]`: one entry per current project with a completion, by name; `weeks` newest first, only weeks with a completion; `estimatedMinutes` is the part of `minutes` that comes from estimates; each week is rounded to whole minutes and the totals are the sums of the rounded weeks) read with one query per project on `projectId` (no index needed) of at most 5000 rows each, and `projectHoursTruncated` when any project had more | No |
| `updateCompletedTask` | Body `{id, updatedFields}`, `id` the `archiveId` each `getCompletedTasks` entry carries. Corrects the archive row only (`applyCompletedEdit`): `priority`, `estimatedMinutes` (1 to 10000; the History form does not send it), `trackedSeconds` (0 to 1000 hours; 0 makes the estimate count), `tags` (cleaned, at most 20), `travelKm` (kilometres, as a task's; null, empty or 0 removes it, 400 for an unusable value), `location` (as a task's, `normalizeLocation`; null, blank text or an empty object removes it, 400 for an unusable value), `phone` and `email` (as a task's; null or empty removes them, 400 for an unusable value), `materials` (a list of texts or of `{text, cost?}` objects, each text trimmed and cut to 100 characters, blanks dropped, at most 30, a cost kept when usable; null or an empty list removes them; stored as a task's materials are, every item checked), `description` (as a task's: null or empty removes it; an older page's non-empty `comments` text counts as it), `projectId` (a current project, or null for none, which also drops `projectOrder`; moving to another project drops it too), and the completion time as `completedDate` plus `completedTime` in the owner's zone (not in the future, not before 2000). Other keys are ignored. 400 with the reason for an unusable value or nothing to change, 404 for an unknown id; responds `{task}` | No |
| `reopenCompletedTask` | Body `{id}`, the `archiveId`. Puts a completed task back on the queue (`reopenTask`) and deletes its archive row in one transaction, its photos re-keyed to the task (a photo added in that instant is moved just after). The task returns at the end of the list as a plain PENDING task with the row's fields, its own id (a new one if that id is on the list), its `trackedSeconds` and `firstStartedAt` kept, its timer stopped and not active, its schedule fields cleared unless it is `locked` (the next run books it), its project dropped if that project is gone, no `recurrence` (a repeating task's next occurrence was added when it was completed, so the series carries on from that and this is a one-off return of the earlier instance), and its job history (`completedCount`, `recentSeconds`) back to what it was before the completion (`historyBefore`: one fewer completion and its tracked time taken off the samples when it is the last one), the completion being recorded afresh when it is completed again. 400 with the reason for an unusable id or an entry without a title, 404 for an unknown id. Body may also carry `requestId` (as `completeTask`'s: a repeat after a lost reply answers `{success, tasks, task: null, duplicate: true}`). Responds `{tasks, task, serverTime}`. No calendar change, no undo | No |
| `deleteCompletedTask` | Body `{id}`. Deletes that archive row and its photos in one transaction, for good (no undo); 404 for an unknown id. Touches no task, calendar event or history | No |
| `addCompletedPhoto` | Body `{archiveId, image}` for a completed task or `{taskId, image}` for one in the queue (exactly one of the two; `photoOwner`), `image` a base64 JPEG (`normalizePhoto`; a `data:image/jpeg;base64,` prefix is tolerated). The archive row and the photo count are read in one transaction: 404 for an unknown row or task, 400 with the reason for an unusable image or when the task already has 3. Responds `{success, photo: {id, bytes, addedAt}}` | No |
| `getCompletedPhotos` | Body `{archiveId}` or `{taskId}`. Responds `{photos: [{id, data, bytes, addedAt}]}`, oldest first, `data` the base64 JPEG; empty for a row with none. Read-only | No |
| `deleteCompletedPhoto` | Body `{id}`, the photo's id. Deletes it for good; 404 for an unknown id | No |
| `getCompletedTemplates` | Read the newest 1000 `completed_tasks` rows and return `{templates}`: one per distinct title (ignoring case), newest first, at most 500, each `{title, priority, estimatedMinutes, tags, completedAt, completedCount}` plus `recentSeconds`, `recurrence`, `projectId`, `projectOrder`, `materials` (`{text, cost?}` per item) `description`, `travelKm`, `phone`, `email` and `location` when the row had them; no due date, schedule, timer or id (`buildTemplates`). `estimatedMinutes` is the rolling average of the job's tracked times rounded to 5 minutes when any was timed, else the newest row's estimate; `completedCount` is the larger of the newest row's stored count and the number of rows with the title (so rows from before history was kept still count), and `recentSeconds` the newest row's, or for older rows the latest tracked times of rows with the title. Read-only | No |
| `getJobSheet` | Body `{archiveId}` (one completed task), `{projectId}` (a project to date) or `{projectId, weekStart}` (its Monday-to-Sunday week; any date in the week names it); 400 for an unusable body, 404 for an unknown task or project. Responds with the PDF itself (`application/pdf`), the contractor information from settings printed under the "Job sheet" title with a line of space above and below it, then the client's details when the project (the task's own, for a task sheet) has a client (a "CLIENT" label over the name, the client's location text, phone and email that exist), every date and time on the owner's clock: for each completed task its job number ("Job no. 123", left out for a task without one), the time its timer was first started (when it was), its completion time, tracked time (or its estimate marked "est."), materials (texts only), travel (the task's km, "Travel 12.5 km", whether or not it is billed), description and photos (two to a row, oldest entries first, at most 60 a sheet; a note under the title block says how many were left out), oldest first; never priority or tags, since a sheet is client-facing; money (no GST) appears only when an hourly rate applies (the project's client's own `hourlyRate`, 0 included, else `settings.jobSheet.hourlyRate`), any listed task has a material with a cost or any has travel while `settings.jobSheet.travelRate` is set, and then a costed material is listed as "Paint ($45.50)", every task prints "Labour $35.42 · Materials $2.50 · Travel $15.00 · Total $52.92" (labour is the billed time at the rate, marked est. when the time is an estimate, and left out without a rate; materials left out when no material of the task has a cost; travel is the task's km at the travel rate, left out without a distance or a rate), each week section and a project's header print the same sums, the header shows the hourly rate and, when travel is billed, the travel rate ("Travel rate $1.20 per km"), and a project sheet opens with a TOTAL AMOUNT whose breakdown gives the travel with its total distance ("Travel $24.60 (20.5 km)"); every sum adds the tasks' own rounded amounts, so lines add up exactly, while the weekly hours remain the History figures; with neither a rate nor a cost the sheet is as it was; a project sheet groups them by week with each week's hours and the project's total, as the History Project hours section figures them (`est.` part included); it lists at most the newest 1000 entries, with a note when it cuts. A project's rows are read as the History hours are (up to 5000). Read-only | No |
| `exportCompletedCsv` | Body `{from?, to?}`: local dates YYYY-MM-DD in the configured zone, both inclusive, either empty for no limit. Responds with the CSV itself (`text/csv`, UTF-8 with a byte order mark): a header and one line per completed task in the range, oldest first (ties by job number): job number (plain, empty for none), task, project and client names (current; empty for none or a deleted project), completed date on the owner's clock, tracked hours (empty when none was tracked), estimated hours, the materials' texts joined with "; ", and the materials' cost in dollars when any item has one (summed in cents), the travel distance in km (empty for none) and its cost in dollars at `settings.jobSheet.travelRate` (empty without a distance or a rate). 400 for an unusable date, an upside-down range, or a range holding more than 10000 tasks (never a cut file). Read-only; ranged on `completedAt`, so no index | No |
| `exportData` | No body. One pretty-printed JSON document, `{format: "taskhub-next-export", version: 1, exportedAt, tasks, projects, clients, projectTemplates, shoppingList, settings, jobCounter, completedTasks, completedTruncated}`: every active task, every project, every client, every project template, the Miscellaneous list, the settings as `getSettings` returns them (run state included), and the newest 10000 rows of `completed_tasks` newest first with `completedAt` as ISO text (`completedTruncated` when the archive reached that limit). Read-only; the collections are read without a shared snapshot | No |
